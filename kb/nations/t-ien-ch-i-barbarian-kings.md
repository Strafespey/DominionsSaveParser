T'ien Ch'i, Barbarian Kings
The Celestial Empire is crumbling. Constant invasions from the barbaric khans have all but destroyed the Imperial
Bureaucracy. The Imperial family has been replaced by Barbarian Kings. The Imperial Guard is no more and the cavalry of
old has been replaced by skilled barbarian horsemen. Ancestral worship is popular once more and priest-mages of
barbarian heritage who lead the Ancestor Cult are replacing the priests of the Bureaucracy. With the displacement of the eunuchs from
power, conscription has fallen out of use.
Race: Humans
Military: Cavalry, heavy infantry, archers.
Magic: Water, Earth, Death, Nature, Air, Astral and some Glamour, Summon Celestial Beings
Priests: Weak
Buildings: Standard Forts
