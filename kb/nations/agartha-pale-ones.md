Agartha, Pale Ones
In dark caverns under the Roots of the Earth a strange kind of one-eyed humanoids has evolved. Untouched by the sun,
they have become known to humans as the Pale Ones. The Pale Ones are gifted with perfect darkvision and gills and
inhabit underwater rivers and lakes as well as cavernous halls. Pale Ones never stop growing and ancient ones can grow to
huge proportions. The wisest of the Ancient Ones are known as the Oracles, priest-mages of great power. The Oracles are gathered in a
great, cavernous hall where they divine the fate of the Pale Ones.
Race: Pale Ones and troglodytes. Darkvision. Poor amphibians. Extra gold and resources in cave forts.
Military: Light infantry, medium infantry, Wet Ones, huge and sacred Ancient Ones.
Magic: Earth, Fire, Water, Death.
Priests: Powerful
Scales & Blesses: Bless points +2
Buildings: Standard Forts
